  <section class="why-exists">

    <div class="wrap why-exists-wrap">

      <span class="eyebrow">Why this exists</span>

      <p class="why-exists-lede">Every Student Known didn't start as a school-culture initiative. It started with a mental health counselor sitting across from students carrying real trauma, in a rural community with almost nowhere close by to send them for care.</p>

      <p>When the nearest support is an hour's drive away, the adults already in the building become the safety net — whether the school planned for that or not. ESK is how we made that safety net intentional instead of accidental: broadening the circle of trusted adults, tracking who's actually known and who isn't, and treating that connection as protective. Schools using it have seen real movement in the indicators that matter most — attendance, disclosed risk, and students staying enrolled long enough to earn their diploma and change their own trajectory.</p>
